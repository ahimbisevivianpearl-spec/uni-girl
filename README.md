# uni-girl
uni life sucks
### Being a Uni Girl Sucks.

Being a uni girl is smiling for pictures\
while silently falling apart,\
wearing your prettiest outfits\
with the heaviest heart.

It’s counting coins for supper,\
pretending you’re doing okay,\
having a thousand things on your mind\
but no one to tell at the end of the day.

It’s lectures when you’re exhausted,\
deadlines when you need a break,\
trying to build a beautiful future\
while wondering how much more you can take.

It’s missing home but loving your freedom,\
wanting love but fearing the pain,\
giving your heart to the wrong people\
then learning to live with the rain.

It’s watching other girls look happy,\
wondering why your life feels behind,\
forgetting that everyone is fighting battles\
they don't post for the world to find.

It's being broke but looking expensive,\
being heartbroken but looking fine,\
saying “I'm good, just tired”\
when you're barely holding your life in line.

And God, being a uni girl is lonely.\
You can be surrounded and still feel alone,\
craving a hug, a little reassurance,\
somewhere that feels like home.

You're expected to grow, to know who you are,\
to make big decisions with a heart full of doubt.\
You're becoming the woman you've dreamed of being\
while trying not to burn out.

Being a uni girl sucks sometimes.\
Nobody tells you how hard it can be\
to study for a future you hope will be brighter\
when the present is breaking you quietly.

But maybe one day, you'll look back at these years,\
at the tears, the stress, the nights you survived,\
and hug the girl who was just trying her best\
to make it through life.

Until then, here's to the girls who are tired,\
who laugh when they want to cry,\
who are figuring out life on a student budget\
and still dare to dream of the sky.

We're not lazy. We're not ungrateful. We're just tired of being strong.

And sometimes, being a uni girl\
is simply trying to survive\
until the day life finally feels like living. 💗
